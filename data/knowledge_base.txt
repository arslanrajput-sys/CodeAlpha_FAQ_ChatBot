2. CHECKING ACCOUNT
============================================================

Q: How do I open a checking account?
A: You can open a SecureBank checking account online, through the SecureBank Mobile app, or by visiting a branch. You must provide valid identification, contact information, and any information required for identity verification.

Q: What documents are required to open an account?
A: You need a valid government-issued photo ID, proof of address, a valid phone number, and an email address.

Q: Is there a minimum opening deposit?
A: The minimum opening deposit for a standard checking account is $25.

Q: Is there a monthly maintenance fee?
A: The standard checking account has a $10 monthly maintenance fee. The fee is waived if you maintain an average monthly balance of at least $1,000 or receive at least one qualifying direct deposit of $500 or more during the statement cycle.

Q: Can I have more than one checking account?
A: Yes. Customers may open multiple checking accounts, subject to SecureBank account approval.

Q: How do I close my checking account?
A: You may request account closure through customer support or at a branch after all pending transactions have cleared and the balance is zero.

Q: Can I add another person to my checking account?
A: Yes. You may request to add a joint account holder. Both account holders may be required to complete identity verification.

Q: When will my account statement be available?
A: Monthly statements are normally available in online banking within five business days after the statement cycle ends.

3. SAVINGS ACCOUNT

Q: How do I open a savings account?
A: You can open a savings account online, in the SecureBank Mobile app, or at a SecureBank branch.

Q: What is the minimum opening deposit for a savings account?

Q: Does the savings account earn interest?

Q: Is there a monthly savings account fee?

Q: Can I transfer money between checking and savings?

Q: How quickly do transfers between my SecureBank accounts happen?

4. DEBIT CARDS
============================================================

Q: When will I receive my debit card?
A: New debit cards normally arrive within 7 to 10 business days after the account is approved.

Q: How do I activate my debit card?
A: You can activate your debit card in the SecureBank Mobile app, through online banking, or by calling the activation number printed on the card sticker.

Q: How do I change my debit card PIN?
A: You can change your debit card PIN at a SecureBank ATM or through the card management section of the mobile app when available.

Q: What should I do if my debit card is lost or stolen?
A: Lock the card immediately in the SecureBank Mobile app and call the 24/7 lost or stolen card hotline at 1-800-555-0199.

Q: Can I temporarily lock my debit card?
A: Yes. You can temporarily lock and unlock an eligible debit card using the SecureBank Mobile app.

Q: Why was my debit card declined?
A: A card may be declined because of insufficient funds, an incorrect PIN, a card lock, an expired card, a security restriction, or a transaction exceeding your card limit.

Q: What is the daily debit card purchase limit?
A: The standard daily debit card purchase limit is $3,000.

Q: What is the daily ATM withdrawal limit?
A: The standard ATM withdrawal limit is $500 per day.

Q: Can I use my debit card internationally?
A: Yes, where the card network is accepted. International transactions may be subject to additional fees and fraud monitoring.

Q: Is there a foreign transaction fee on debit card purchases?
A: SecureBank charges a 3% foreign transaction fee on eligible debit card purchases made in a foreign currency or processed outside the United States.

Q: How do I dispute a debit card transaction?
A: If you do not recognize a transaction, first lock your card. Then contact SecureBank customer support as soon as possible to start a dispute.

16. SECURITY AND FRAUD
============================================================

Q: I see a transaction I do not recognize. What should I do?
A: Lock the affected card immediately if possible and contact SecureBank customer support. Do not share your password, PIN, or verification codes.

Q: Someone asked me for my one-time verification code. Should I give it to them?
A: No. Never share a one-time verification code with another person. SecureBank will not ask you to provide a one-time code to an unsolicited caller or message sender.

Q: I received a suspicious email claiming to be SecureBank. What should I do?
A: Do not click links or download attachments. Contact SecureBank using the official contact information in this knowledge base.

Q: Will SecureBank ask for my password by email or phone?
A: No. SecureBank will never ask you to reveal your complete online banking password.

Q: How do I protect my account?
A: Use a strong unique password, enable available multi-factor authentication, keep contact information current, monitor transactions, and never share passwords, PINs, or verification codes.

Q: What should I do if my phone is lost?
A: Contact your mobile carrier and SecureBank if you believe your banking information could be at risk. Change your online banking password and review recent transactions.

Q: Can I lock my card from the mobile app?
A: Yes. Eligible debit and credit cards can be temporarily locked through card management.

Q: What should I do if I believe my identity has been stolen?
A: Contact SecureBank customer support immediately, review your accounts, change online banking credentials, and take appropriate identity-protection steps.

Q: How long do pending card transactions take to post?

29. CHATBOT RESPONSE RULES
============================================================

The chatbot should follow these rules:

1. Answer using only information contained in this knowledge base.
2. Keep answers short, clear, and customer-friendly.
3. Do not invent account balances, transaction details, approval decisions, interest rates, or customer-specific information.
4. Never ask the user to provide:
   - Full debit or credit card numbers
   - Complete Social Security numbers
   - Online banking passwords
   - PIN numbers
   - One-time verification codes
5. For lost or stolen cards, tell the user to lock the card and call:
   1-800-555-0199
6. For suspected fraud, advise the user to secure the account and contact SecureBank.
7. If a user asks for something outside this knowledge base, respond:
   "Sorry, I could not find an answer to that question. Please contact SecureBank customer support."
8. The chatbot must not claim it completed actions such as:
   - Sending money
   - Closing an account
   - Blocking a card
   - Approving a loan
   - Refunding a transaction
   - Changing customer information
9. The chatbot may explain how the user can perform an action, but should not claim to perform it.
10. For emergencies involving fraud or a lost/stolen card, prioritize security instructions.
